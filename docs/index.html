Overview

GRAVITY FLOW is a single-player physics puzzle game. We designed it to respect your privacy: your gameplay stays on your device, and we collect only the limited data needed to keep the app stable, understand general usage, and show optional ads. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not require you to create an account or sign in.

This policy explains what is collected, by whom, and why.

Information stored only on your device (not collected by us)

The following is saved locally on your device (browser/app localStorage) and is never transmitted to us:

  • Level progress, unlocked levels, and star ratings
  • Best times and "ghost" replay paths
  • In-game currency (Stardust, Cosmic Fragments) and owned/equipped cosmetics
  • Daily-challenge streaks and achievements
  • Settings (sound, music, haptics, reduced-motion)

Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes this information.

Information processed by third-party services

To operate the app we use the following established third-party SDKs. Each processes a limited set of data under its own privacy policy:

ServiceData processedPurpose
Google Firebase Analytics App activity (events, sessions) and device / other identifiers Understand general, aggregated usage so we can improve the game
Google Firebase Crashlytics Crash logs and diagnostic information Diagnose and fix stability problems (app functionality)
Google AdMob Device and advertising identifiers, approximate usage signals Show ads — rewarded ads are opt-in and interstitials are frequency-capped; data is shared with Google for ad delivery
RevenueCat Purchase and transaction information (no card details) Manage in-app purchases and the "Remove Ads" entitlement

We do not control how Google or RevenueCat process data on their own platforms. Please review their policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from sites/apps that use its services
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy

Ads

Ads are served by Google AdMob. Rewarded ads are always optional and are never required to progress through the game; interstitial ads are shown infrequently (rate-limited) and are suppressed entirely for players who purchase Remove Ads. Where required by law (for example in the EEA/UK), we present a consent request before serving personalized ads, managed through Google's User Messaging Platform.

Purchases

In-app purchases (e.g. Remove Ads and optional cosmetic bundles) are processed by the Google Play billing system; RevenueCat manages the resulting entitlement. We never receive or store your payment-card details — those are handled by Google Play. All purchasable content is cosmetic (or the optional Remove-Ads convenience). There is no pay-to-win.

Children

GRAVITY FLOW is rated for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not use data to serve personalized ads to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will address it.

Data retention and deletion

Gameplay data stored on your device remains there until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. We do not keep a separate server-side copy of that on-device data.

Information processed by the third-party services described above is retained and deleted in accordance with each provider's own policies and controls — we do not directly control or store that data. If you wish to access or delete information held by those providers, we will assist you and direct you to the appropriate provider and tools (for example, your device's advertising-identifier controls, or your Google Play account settings). You may also contact us at truestorylabs@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable period and help where we can.

We may retain certain information for longer where reasonably necessary to comply with legal obligations, prevent fraud or abuse, maintain the security and integrity of the app, resolve disputes, or enforce our terms — or for other legitimate business purposes. Once it is no longer needed for these purposes, such information is deleted or anonymized.

Security

Data transmitted to the third-party services above is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we rely on reputable providers and minimize the data involved.

International users

The third-party providers may process data on servers located outside your country, including the United States. By using the app you understand that data may be transferred and processed in those locations under the providers' safeguards.
