## Overview

GRAVITY FLOW is a single-player physics puzzle game. We designed it to respect your privacy:
your gameplay stays on your device, and we collect only the limited data needed to keep the
app stable, understand general usage, and show optional ads. We do **not** sell your personal
data, and we do **not** require you to create an account or sign in.

This policy explains what is collected, by whom, and why.

## Information stored only on your device (not collected by us)

The following is saved locally on your device (browser/app `localStorage`) and is **never
transmitted to us**:

- Level progress, unlocked levels, and star ratings
- Best times and "ghost" replay paths
- In-game currency (Stardust, Cosmic Fragments) and owned/equipped cosmetics
- Daily-challenge streaks and achievements
- Settings (sound, music, haptics, reduced-motion)

Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes this information.

## Information processed by third-party services

To operate the app we use the following established third-party SDKs. Each processes a limited
set of data under its own privacy policy:

| Service | Data processed | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Google Firebase Analytics** | App activity (events, sessions) and device / other identifiers | Understand general, aggregated usage so we can improve the game |
| **Google Firebase Crashlytics** | Crash logs and diagnostic information | Diagnose and fix stability problems (app functionality) |
| **Google AdMob** | Device and advertising identifiers, approximate usage signals | Show ads — **rewarded ads are opt-in** and **interstitials are frequency-capped**; data is shared with Google for ad delivery |
| **RevenueCat** | Purchase and transaction information (no card details) | Manage in-app purchases and the "Remove Ads" entitlement |

We do not control how Google or RevenueCat process data on their own platforms. Please review
their policies:

- Google Privacy Policy — https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses data from sites/apps that use its services — https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- RevenueCat Privacy Policy — https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

## Ads

Ads are served by Google AdMob. Rewarded ads are always **optional** and are never required to
progress through the game; interstitial ads are shown infrequently (rate-limited) and are
suppressed entirely for players who purchase **Remove Ads**. Where required by law (for example
in the EEA/UK), we present a **consent request** before serving personalized ads, managed
through Google's User Messaging Platform.

## Purchases

In-app purchases (e.g. Remove Ads and optional cosmetic bundles) are processed by the Google
Play billing system; RevenueCat manages the resulting entitlement. We never receive or store
your payment-card details — those are handled by Google Play. All purchasable content is
**cosmetic** (or the optional Remove-Ads convenience). There is no pay-to-win.

## Children

GRAVITY FLOW is rated for a general audience and is **not directed at children under 13**. We
do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not use data to serve
personalized ads to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact
us and we will address it.

## Data retention and deletion

Locally stored gameplay data lives on your device until you clear it or uninstall the app. Data
processed by the third parties above is retained per their respective policies. You may request
deletion of any data associated with you by emailing **truestorylabs@gmail.com**; we will respond
within a reasonable period and coordinate with the relevant provider where applicable.

## Security

Data transmitted to the third-party services above is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). No
method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we rely on reputable providers and
minimize the data involved.

## International users

The third-party providers may process data on servers located outside your country, including
the United States. By using the app you understand that data may be transferred and processed in
those locations under the providers' safeguards.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves (for example, if integrations change). Material
changes will be reflected here with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after an
update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
