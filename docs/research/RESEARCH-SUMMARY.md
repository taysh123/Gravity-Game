| Bosses, finale, remix | [Ramachandran](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/opinion-boss-design---trial-punishment); [Prankster Comets](https://www.mariowiki.com/Prankster_Comet) | Same verb, higher stakes, new composition. | QA bot | [King CIG 2018](https://www.gwern.net/doc/reinforcement-learning/imitation-learning/2018-gudmundsson.pdf); [Rovio 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.12061); [Tactile 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.14626); [PHYRE](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.05656) | Agents estimate difficulty in under a minute, vs 7 days of human playtests.
