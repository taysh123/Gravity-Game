# Rename research: replacing "GRAVITY FLOW" (screening as of 2026-10-07)

> **Not legal advice.** This is a knockout screen to narrow the options. It is not trademark clearance.
> **SCREENING RESULT** = what a query actually returned. **OPINION** = my judgment as a brand/naming strategist.

## 0. Method and limits

- **Trademarks.** USPTO: I queried the `tmsearch.uspto.gov` backend directly (exact phrase, wildcard and fuzzy matching; live marks flagged when they fall in IC 9, 28 or 41). EU/WIPO: **TMview** (tmdn.org), which pulls in EUIPO (EM), WIPO Madrid (WO) and about 70 national offices, run in "contains" and fuzzy modes. EUIPO eSearch and the WIPO Global Brand Database only work in a browser with JavaScript, so I did not query them directly. TMview covers their records.
- **Stores.** Google Play search pages (US), the Apple iTunes Search API (US store) and Steam store search. **itch.io search returned only the homepage to a non-JS fetch, so it is inconclusive.**
- **Domains.** RDAP lookups on the registry servers (Verisign for .com, Google for .app, CentralNic for .game). A 404 means no registration record, which usually means the domain is available. A 200 means it is registered.
- **Handles.** I loaded YouTube `@handle`, TikTok `@handle` and GitHub user URLs directly. On TikTok, `statusCode 10221` means the account does not exist. **I could not check Instagram or X without logging in.**
- **Web search.** The session's web-search budget ran out after 3 queries, so no `site:` searches were possible. Every check above was a direct fetch from the first-party source instead.

## 1. The "Gravity Flow" conflict

**SCREENING RESULT**
- **USPTO:** **GRAVITY FLOW, Reg. No. 8,281,133** (Principal Register, registered **2026-06-02**; SN 99451755, filed 2025-10-20, basis 1(a) actual use). Owner: **Rocketgenius, Inc.** It covers **IC 9** (downloadable plugin software that automates business processes on websites) and **IC 42** (SaaS). Rocketgenius's 2018 registrations, 5,579,783 (GRAVITY FLOW) and 5,560,409 (GRAVITYFLOW), are now **CANCELLED**, and the company **re-filed in 2025**. That shows it actively maintains the mark.
- **No GRAVITY FLOW mark exists in US IC 28 or 41.** Elsewhere (TMview):
  - JP "GRAVITY FLOW DESIGN", class 28, registered (Mizuno)
  - CH "GravityFLOW", classes 1/7/9, registered (InSphero)
  - ES "Gravity Flow Yoga", class 41
  - GB, class 11; MX, class 20
  - No EUTM.
- **Google Play ("gravity flow"):**
  - An exact **"Gravity Flow"** by IACG MULTIMEDIA PRIVATE LIMITED (`com.shakeer.gravityflow`, Casual game, 5+ downloads, updated 2026-09-03)
  - **"Gravity Flow: Happy Cup"** by Haiyi Xiao (`com.gp.gravityflowhappycup`, Casual, **10K+**)
  - Also "Gravity Ink Flow", "Gravity Flux" and "Flow Merge: Gravity Puzzle"
- **Other stores:** Apple's US store has no "Gravity Flow" title. Steam has no exact match. A web search before the budget ran out found an itch.io demo called "Gravity Flow – Enhanced Edition".
- **Domains:**
  - gravityflow.io is Rocketgenius's product site.
  - gravityflow.com (registered 1999) belongs to "Gravity Flow Systems Southwest" (solid waste).
  - gravityflow.app is registered (2018).
  - gravityflow.game returns 404.
- **Handles:** TikTok @gravityflow and YouTube @gravityflow are both taken.

**OPINION: risk**
- **(a) Keep "GRAVITY FLOW."** Legal risk is **Medium**; brand/ASO risk is **High**.
  - The mark is identical and registered in the same class (IC 9 software). The goods differ, though (WordPress workflow plugin vs. game), so a real confusion claim is not the most likely outcome.
  - The bigger problems are practical:
    1. We almost certainly **could not register** GRAVITY FLOW ourselves in IC 9. A 2(d) refusal over Reg. 8,281,133 is likely, which leaves us with no protection.
    2. The owner actively polices and re-files its mark, so a cease-and-desist letter is unlikely but possible.
    3. Two Play games already carry the name, so it has **zero store distinctiveness**.
    4. gravityflow.io dominates search results.
- **(b) Qualify it ("Gravity Flow: Star Puzzle").** Risk is essentially unchanged: **Medium / High.** Adding descriptive words doesn't change the dominant element of the mark. It also doesn't fix registrability or discoverability.
- **Recommendation:** rename **before** the public Play launch, while it is cheapest.

## 5. Top 3

**1. HOMING STAR** (OPINION: best balance)
- **Store title:** `Homing Star: Gravity Puzzle` (27/30)
- **Tagline:** *"Hold to pull. Bring the lost star home."*
- **Why:**
  - It is the cleanest result on every database I checked.
  - It is made of two easy words that people worldwide can pronounce.
  - It fuses the mechanic ("homing" means being pulled toward a target) with the existing story.
  - The current tagline and lore carry over unchanged. Zero rewrite.
  - Wordmark: Orbitron caps, with the "O" of HOMING drawn as the lensing ring.
- **Watch:**
  - "Homing missile" connotation. The cosmic art and tagline outweigh it.
  - .com isn't available, so lead with **homingstar.app** / **playhomingstar.com**.
  - Two dictionary words give a narrower, "suggestive" mark: registrable, but with less scope than a coined word.

**2. ORBITIDE** (OPINION: most ownable)
- **Store title:** `Orbitide: Gravity Puzzle` (24/30)
- **Tagline:** *"Ride the pull. Bring the lost star home."*
- **Why:**
  - A coined word gives the strongest trademark and a search term no one else owns.
  - 8 characters is ideal for the icon and splash.
  - "Tide" is a graceful evolution from "Flow", so existing copy like "Find the flow" still rhymes with the brand.
  - TikTok @orbitide is free.
- **Watch:**
  - Pronunciation (OR-bih-tide vs. or-BIT-ide; the "-ide" ending can read as chemical). The splash/voice can teach it.
  - Counsel needs to review the EU **ORBITUDE** class 9 filing.
  - The .com belongs to someone (registered 2025).

**3. WAYWARD STAR** (OPINION: best story)
- **Store title:** `Wayward Star: Gravity Puzzle` (28/30)
- **Tagline:** *"One lost star. Your gravity. The way home."*
- **Why:**
  - Most emotional of the three, and it matches the premium-puzzler tone (Alto, Monument Valley).
  - Zero hits in both the US and TMview databases.
- **Watch:**
  - "Wayward" is a common prefix among Steam space games.
  - waywardstar.com was registered in April 2026 and is parked. Check that no game launches under the name.
  - It says nothing about the mechanic.

*Honorable mention:* **Pullaris**. It is the best literal fit for the verb, but the exact-name agency and the pull of the Polaris brand make it a weaker #4.

*Tagline note:* USPTO has **THE LOST STAR** (IC 9/41, music; Reg. 5,353,041). Using "Bring the lost star home" descriptively as a tagline is likely fine (OPINION). Mention it to counsel anyway.

## 6. What changes on rename, and what must not

**Changes (user-visible):**
- **Display name:**
  - `capacitor.config.ts` → `appName`
  - `android/app/src/main/res/values/strings.xml` → `app_name`, `title_activity_main`
  - `src/config/splash.config.ts` → `GAME_TITLE`
  - `src/utils/Share.ts` → default share title
  - `index.html` → `<title>` (currently "Project Gravity")
- **Logo/wordmark:**
  - `assets/images/gravity-flow-logo.png` and its raw source (re-run `npm run optimize:assets`)
  - Shown in the IntroSplash logo reveal and MainMenu. The internal `IMAGES.gravityFlowLogo` key can stay.
- **Store:**
  - Play title/short/full description, ASO keywords, release notes
  - Privacy policy app name and `docs/index.html` (hosted page)
  - Feature graphic, icon (if it carries text), and any screenshot showing the logo (the main menu shot)
- **Third-party dashboards:** Firebase, AdMob and RevenueCat display names. Cosmetic only.
- **Docs:** README, CLAUDE.md, `docs/STATUS.md`, docs/store/*.
- **Stays as-is:** the in-game "FLOW" streak tier and "Find the flow" copy. "Flow" alone is a generic word.

**Must not change:**
- **`applicationId` / namespace `com.truestorylabs.gravityflow`.** It is permanent once any AAB has been uploaded to Play Console, even to internal testing. Users only see it in the Play URL, and legacy IDs are common and harmless.
- The `package_name` / `custom_url_scheme` strings and the Java package path. They are tied to the ID.
- Signing keystore.
- Firebase project ID / `google-services.json`, plus the AdMob and RevenueCat app IDs. None of these are user-facing.

## 7. Recommended decision process

1. **Owner picks** a primary and a backup from the top 3. Test them by saying each aloud, mocking up the Orbitron icon, and reading the store title on a phone.
2. **Re-check knockouts the same day.** Names move fast: starhold.app was registered in June 2026, and waywardstar.com in April 2026.
3. **Professional clearance** by a trademark attorney:
   - Comprehensive search covering registers, common-law use and app stores
   - Classes **9 and 41** (28 optional)
   - **US, EU and UK** at minimum, plus any launch markets (CA/AU; JP/KR if targeted)
4. **Secure assets the moment you decide, before announcing:**
   - Domains: .app, .game, play-/get- variants (and the .com if it can be bought)
   - Handles: the `@<name>game` variants that are free on YouTube and TikTok
   - Check Instagram and X manually
5. **File** a US intent-to-use application (1(b)) and/or an EUTM in classes 9 and 41. Extend through Madrid later.
6. **Then rename** in code, assets and the store (§6), and launch under the new name.
